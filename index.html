Homepage review: sidi.io

October 2026 · For Cody and the Sidi team · Checked on desktop (1440px) and mobile (390px)

The page converts warm traffic that already knows Sidi. It does little for people who don't: there is nothing for Google to rank, and most of the trust Sidi has earned is either missing or hidden from search engines. Below is what is broken, how it affects real visitors, and how to fix it.

At a glance

  • The site is one page. sidi.io/services, /about, /blog and /contact all return 404.
  • robots.txt and sitemap.xml are empty files (0 bytes).
  • 25 H1 tags on the homepage. There should be one.
  • No menu on mobile. Desktop has 3 jump links.
  • Reviews load through a widget, so Google and AI tools don't see them.
  • Riaz, the team, the Ottawa office and the awards are never named on the page.

What already works (keep it)

  • Case studies show real ad creatives next to real numbers.
  • The founder video puts a face on the agency.
  • FAQ answers are clear and specific (60-day notice, client owns ad accounts).

What's broken

01 · SEO

There are no pages beyond the homepage

  • Every service Sidi sells (Google Ads, SEO, social ads, GEO) gets one line at most. None has its own URL.
  • /services, /about, /blog and /contact return a 404 page.

Example: someone in Ottawa searches "Google Ads agency Ottawa". Google shows competitors' Google Ads pages. Sidi has no page about Google Ads, so it can't appear. If that person types sidi.io/services, they get the screen on the right.

Fix: a page for each service, plus About, Team, Contact and Case Studies, all linked from a real menu.

sidi.io/services today
02 · UX

Mobile visitors have no menu

  • On a phone the header is the logo only. On desktop it's 3 jump links: How It Works, Proof, FAQ.
  • The mobile page is about 9,400px tall.

Example: a marketing manager opens the site from a LinkedIn post on their phone to check if Sidi does SEO. There is no menu and no services section. They either scroll the whole page or leave.

Fix: a header with a menu on every screen size (see the mockup), with phone number and "Book a call" always visible.

Mobile, first screen
03 · Messaging

The first screen doesn't say what Sidi does

  • At 1440x900 the "Schedule a call" button is below the fold. The visitor sees a headline and a video.
  • The headline is about wasted ad spend. It doesn't mention services, Ottawa or Canada.
  • The title tag says "Demand Generation Agency For The Bold", the meta description talks about healthcare and B2B, and the H1 talks about ad spend. Three different positions.

Example: a business owner comparing three agencies gives each homepage a few seconds. On Sidi's they can't tell what is offered or where Sidi is based without pressing play or scrolling.

Fix: one line that says what Sidi is and where (performance marketing agency, Ottawa). Button and review rating visible without scrolling. Same line in the title tag and meta description.

Desktop, first screen (1440x900)
04 · E-E-A-T

The reviews are real, but search engines can't see them

  • The 4 Google reviews (Perley Health Foundation, Sedation Dental Group, Scrivens, Résidence Le Parc) are loaded by a third-party script. They are not in the page HTML.
  • When the script loads slowly, the section shows only the heading and a button. That is how it appeared in our first screenshot.
  • Four long white cards take about 1,600px. There is no overall rating (5.0 from 155 reviews).

Example: when ChatGPT or Google's AI Overview looks for well-reviewed marketing agencies in Ottawa, it reads the page HTML. Sidi's reviews aren't in it.

Fix: rating summary plus 3 short quotes in plain HTML, with name, role and company. Link to the full Google profile.

Reviews section, once the widget loads
05 · Content

"How We Help You Win" could be on any agency's site

  • "A partner, not just a vendor", "No fluff", "world-class communication": no proof attached to any of them.
  • "70% less than hiring a team" has no source.
  • Centered paragraphs are slow to read.

Fix: replace it with a services grid linking to each service page. Move the promises that matter (no markup, you own the accounts) into a short comparison with a typical agency and an in-house hire.

How We Help You Win
06 · Trust

Strong client logos, with no names attached

  • Carleton University, Algonquin College, Calian, SaaS North and others scroll by in a carousel with no label.
  • Every logo has the alt text "Logo", so search engines don't know who they are.

Fix: label the strip ("Brands we've worked with"), name each logo in its alt text, and add the case-study brands (Fence-All, Lanark Lifestyles).

Logo carousel
07 · Conversion

The form asks for a lot and doesn't say what happens next

  • 5 required fields: company, job title, name, email, phone.
  • Nothing about what happens after submitting, or how fast Sidi replies.
  • Two buttons with different labels ("Schedule a call" and "Schedule call"), one above the other.

Example: the owner of a 10-person fencing company has to invent a job title. Someone who only wants to learn more has to hand over a phone number first. Both are common reasons people abandon a form.

Fix: two steps. First website, monthly budget and service needed, then name, email and optional phone. Drop job title. Add "We reply within 1 business day".

Closing section and form
08 · E-E-A-T

No people, no address, no awards

  • Riaz is in the video but never named. There is no team section.
  • No Ottawa address on the page or in the schema.
  • Ottawa Fastest Growing Companies (2022, 2023, 2024) isn't mentioned.

Fix: a team section (Riaz plus 3 leads, with LinkedIn), awards and partner badges, and the full address in the footer and schema.

09 · Technical

Code-level problems

  • 25 H1 tags. Every heading and every stat is an H1, and the hero headline is in the HTML twice.
  • robots.txt and sitemap.xml return empty files. No canonical tag.
  • 13 images have empty alt text.
  • A broken character in "you've" inside the schema markup.
  • "og:keywords" and "og:author" are not real tags and do nothing.
  • Schema has no address, no founder, and no links to the Google, LinkedIn or DesignRush profiles.

Fix: one H1, H2 per section, H3 per card. Real robots.txt and sitemap. Canonical. Organization schema with address, founder and profile links.
